# Talk It Initiative — Website Sitemap

An information website for Talk It Initiative. The site explains who the organization is, what it does, and how people can get involved. There are no member portals, dashboards, or authenticated areas — public pages and simple contact/signup forms only.

Audiences:

- Young people looking to participate
- Volunteers looking to contribute
- Donors and sponsors evaluating credibility
- Partner organizations considering collaboration
- Media looking for information
- Governments and NGOs seeking impact data
